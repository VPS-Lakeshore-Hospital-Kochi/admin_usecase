Using Claude safely at VPS Lakeshore

How non-clinical departments can adopt Claude with a named approver on every output, no patient data at risk, and a clear order of rollout — not a legal document, and not a substitute for Legal, Compliance or IT Security sign-off.

Principles

Human in the loop

Claude drafts; a named person approves. Nothing posts to HIS, ERP or finance systems automatically — every output is reviewed and entered by a human before it takes effect.

No patient-identifiable data in prototypes

These pages run on synthetic or anonymised sample data only. In production, patient data flows only through an approved, logged hospital gateway — never pasted into a public AI tool.

Accuracy

Outputs cite the input they were drawn from. Figures are re-checked against source before anyone relies on them for a decision.

Accountability

Every use case has a named business owner (accountable for the decision) and a data owner (accountable for what data feeds it).

Transparency

Staff and recipients know when a letter, note or reply was AI-drafted. It is disclosed, not passed off as fully manual work.

Scope

This hub covers non-clinical use only. Clinical decision support is a separate, clinically governed track — see below.

Approval tiers

TIER 1 Drafting help

Low risk — text drafts, summaries, Q&A. Approved by the team lead before use.

TIER 2 Decision support on money or people

Capex, vendor selection, hiring, attrition. Approved by the department head, with Finance or HR as relevant.

TIER 3 Patient data or external parties

Anything touching patient-identifiable data, insurers, or overseas parties. Approved by IT Security, Legal and Quality together.
